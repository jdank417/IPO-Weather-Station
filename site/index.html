Volatility Surface Sculptor

Each trading day, the VIX term structure (9 days to 6 months) is read as a Heston variance forecast. That fixes three of the model's five parameters. Drag the sliders to reshape the implied-volatility surface those parameters produce. The other two are yours to choose.

Roadmap

  1. Repo scaffolding and Pages deploy
  2. Cboe VIX term-structure fetcher and daily Heston calibration
  3. In-browser Heston / COS pricer and implied-vol solver
  4. Interactive volatility surface with sliders
  5. IPO regime model: Ritter and FRED data, Markov switching with time-varying transitions
  6. Hot-IPO gauge with what-if links to the surface

Model: Heston (1993). Characteristic function in the Albrecher et al. (2007) form, priced with the COS method (Fang & Oosterlee 2008), all in your browser. Index data: Cboe Global Markets, used to fit parameters; raw data is not redistributed. Not investment advice. Source and docs.
