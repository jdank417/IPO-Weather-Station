IPO Weather Station

How hot is the IPO market, given today's fear and inflation?

A static, client-side model that reads the VIX term structure as a Heston variance forecast, sculpts the implied-volatility surface it implies, and (in Stage 2) maps volatility and inflation onto the probability of a hot IPO regime.

  6. Hot-IPO gauge with what-if links to the surface
