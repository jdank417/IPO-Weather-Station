Is the IPO window open?

Companies go public in waves: some months are busy ("hot"), others quiet ("cold"). This page reads how nervous the stock market is today, and how much inflation investors expect, and turns that into a forecast for next month. It updates every trading day.

What's a "hot" month?

In a hot IPO market many more companies go public each month than in a cold one.

    Landscape shape

    These two shape the landscape's tilt and curvature. The VIX readings can't pin them down, and they don't affect the IPO forecast. Defaults: 0.6 and −0.7.

    The fear landscape

    How to read it: height and color show how much turbulence the options market prices in (implied volatility). Front to back is time, from 1 week to 2 years. Left to right is price level: big drops on the left, big rises on the right. A higher left side means investors pay more to protect against a crash.

    Technical: Heston implied volatility; strikes in standard deviations of ln(K/F) at today's vol (−2.5 to +2). Gaps mark prices too small to resolve. Drag to rotate.

    For analysts: model coefficients and comparison

    Two-regime Markov-switching model of log(1 + net IPOs) with logistic time-varying transition probabilities (Diebold, Lee & Weinbach 1994; Filardo 1994), estimated by maximum likelihood. Covariates are last month's values, standardized. Full write-up in docs/regime_model.rst.

    Plain-English glossary

    IPO
    Initial public offering: a company selling shares on the stock market for the first time. Counts here exclude SPACs, penny stocks and similar listings.
    Hot / cold IPO market
    Two states the model finds in the data: busy periods with many listings, and quiet ones. Markets tend to stay in one state for months.
    VIX
    Cboe's "fear index": the stock-price turbulence that S&P 500 options imply over the next 30 days. VIX9D, VIX3M and VIX6M do the same for 9 days, 3 months and 6 months.
    Volatility
    How much prices are expected to swing over a year, in percent. 16% means a typical yearly move of about 16%.
    SKEW
    Cboe index of how expensive crash protection is compared with ordinary protection. Higher means more worry about a sudden fall.
    Breakeven inflation
    The average inflation rate bond investors expect over the next 5 years, read from the gap between ordinary and inflation-protected Treasury yields.
    Heston model
    A standard model in which volatility drifts back toward a long-run level at a certain speed and is itself random. It turns a few readings into a whole landscape of option prices.
