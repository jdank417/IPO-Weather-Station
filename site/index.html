Is the IPO window open?

Each trading day, the VIX term structure is read as a Heston variance forecast, and a regime model links that volatility, crash fear (SKEW) and inflation expectations to the odds that next month is a hot IPO month. Reshape the volatility surface below and watch the forecast respond.

    Model coefficients and comparison

    Two-regime Markov-switching model of log(1 + net IPOs) with logistic time-varying transition probabilities (Diebold, Lee & Weinbach 1994; Filardo 1994), estimated by maximum likelihood. Covariates are last month's values, standardized. Full write-up in docs/regime_model.rst.

    Built in six milestones

    1. Repo scaffolding and Pages deploy
    2. Cboe VIX term-structure fetcher and daily Heston calibration
    3. In-browser Heston / COS pricer and implied-vol solver
    4. Interactive volatility surface with sliders
    5. IPO regime model: Ritter and FRED data, Markov switching with time-varying transitions
    6. Hot-IPO gauge with what-if links to the surface

    Model: Heston (1993). Characteristic function in the Albrecher et al. (2007) form, priced with the COS method (Fang & Oosterlee 2008), all in your browser. Index data: Cboe Global Markets, used to fit parameters; raw data is not redistributed. IPO data: Jay R. Ritter, University of Florida (vintage through December 2025). Inflation: Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis, FRED. Not investment advice. Source and docs.
